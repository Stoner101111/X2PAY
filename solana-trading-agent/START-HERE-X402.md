## 🎯 **What You Can Do Right Now**

### **1. Generate Payment Requirements** 💳
- Go to "🚀 x402 Payments - Tap to Pay" section
- Select network: Base Sepolia
- Enter amount: 10.00
- Enter your address: 0x...
- Click "Generate Payment Requirements"
- **Get JSON** with complete payment requirements!

### **2. Connect Bluetooth Device** 📡
- Scroll to "📡 Tap-to-Pay Scanner"
- Click "Scan for Devices"
- Select your x402 device
- **View real-time payment info!**

### **3. Check Pricing Models** 💡
- Click "💡 Pricing Strategy" in header
- Compare Freemium, Transaction, Hybrid
- Analyze customer segments
- **Review strategic roadmap!**
