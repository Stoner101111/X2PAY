================================================================================
  🌍 DEPLOY YOUR PORTAL TO VERCEL - STEP BY STEP
================================================================================

Portal is ready! Follow these steps to make it live at:
https://x4pay-portal.vercel.app

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN YOUR BROWSER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your browser should have opened to:
https://vercel.com/oauth/device?user_code=VZXV-MRWM

If not, manually visit that URL in your browser.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: LOGIN TO VERCEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Click "Continue with GitHub" or create account
2. Authorize Vercel access
3. Approve device authentication

The browser will show "Device approved" when done.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: RETURN TO POWERSHELL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once authenticated in browser, press ENTER in PowerShell.

Then run:
  vercel

Answer the prompts:
  Set up and deploy? → Y
  Which scope? → Your account
  Link to existing? → N
  Project name? → x4pay-portal
  Override settings? → N

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: GET YOUR URL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Vercel will deploy and give you:
https://x4pay-portal.vercel.app

Your portal is now LIVE! 🎉

================================================================================

Quick Commands:
  vercel            - Deploy
  vercel --prod     - Deploy to production
  vercel logs       - View logs
